Xerox today introduced the Xerox® C240/C245, Xerox® C255a, and Xerox® C300/C303a/C305ae color printers and multifunction printers, the first hardware offerings launched under the unified Xerox brand following the company's integration with Lexmark. Xerox now operates as a single technology company spanning print, IT infrastructure and intelligent digital services. The updated Xerox brand identity, including the refreshed logo, reflects the next phase of transformation into a stronger, more competitive technology company.
