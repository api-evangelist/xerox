The New York Jets have partnered with Xerox, one of the world’s most recognized technology leaders, in a new multi-year agreement focused on integrating technology across the team’s day-to-day football and business operations. As a central part of the relationship, Xerox solutions are being implemented throughout the organization, including document management, printing infrastructure, and internal workflows across both the training facility and front office. “We’re excited to welcome Xerox into the Jets family,” said Jeff Fernandez, Jets Senior Vice President of Business Development + Venture
